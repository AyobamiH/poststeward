and checking. After the owner replaced the draft with an explicit synthetic

a readability benefit from retained generation annotations to text that no

## Correction

Checking previously received the original strategy and each draft's generated
rationale and claim annotations alongside owner-edited text. Those annotations
remain useful provenance but can describe removed assertions. The check phase now
receives only each current draft's alias and exact text, with approved context,
pinned evidence, source scope, coverage and provider identity. The current
strategy's audience and objective remain as review intent; its generated facts
and positioning do not become assertions attributed to publication text. Draft generation
still receives its strategy. The original annotations remain stored and exported;
the owner view labels them separately in a disclosure explaining their basis.

Editorial policy `2026-10-01-current-copy-v3` asks the checker to examine every
assertion in current copy, retain accurate synthetic qualifications, identify
concrete violated constraints and give actionable corrections. A valid single
Threads/X text block is not required to be multipart. The existing critique gate
does not filter or override model issues. Quotes, private-source disclosure,
token/call/spending limits, exact revision/digest approval and account fencing
remain enforced. No automatic paid recheck or owner approval occurs on deployment.

## Validation boundary

Controlled regression tests inspect real model request material before and after
owner edits, preserve stored annotations, exercise an unsupported assertion in
current copy and verify it still prevents approval. Browser checks distinguish
editable text from original notes and retain blocking controls. These checks prove
input separation and gate enforcement, not live model judgement.

Live acceptance requires a separately authorised check of the owner's saved
copy after the new release is deployed. Inspect its recorded policy/release and
critique. Do not repeat paid calls or remove blockers to manufacture a pass. The
private synthetic fixture is not a public release announcement.
