Most social tools solve the calendar.

That is not the part that keeps stopping me.

The hard part is opening tomorrow’s calendar and still needing something worth saying.

OneClickPostFactory is being built around that problem:

capture useful source material,
bank reusable angles,
fill the queue from what you already know.

The goal isn’t “AI writes more posts.”

The goal is to stop valuable ideas from disappearing while the content calendar goes empty.

That is the factory I want.
