A clean UI can make a workflow look finished long before the operating system underneath it is ready.

That is one of the biggest lessons I keep seeing when moving Lovable-built ideas toward production.

The visible flow is only the start.

Then come the parts users actually depend on:

who owns the credentials,
what happens when a job fails,
what state is safe to retry,
what the history says happened,
and whether one user can ever see another user’s state.

I wrote about building a Reddit-to-content workflow for Lovable builders because that gap matters.

The interesting part is not “AI can write a post.”

It is turning useful source material into a controlled workflow where review, state and consequences stay visible.

That is the standard I want OneClickPostFactory to meet as it moves from builder project to usable system.
