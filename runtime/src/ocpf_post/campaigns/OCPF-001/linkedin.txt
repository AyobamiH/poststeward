A useful Reddit answer should not die in one thread.

Founders already create good source material every week: a detailed answer, a debugging lesson, a customer explanation, a product decision.

Then the idea gets used once and disappears.

I’m building OneClickPostFactory around a different model: one real source becomes reusable content inventory.

The source is kept. Multiple angles are extracted. Each network gets its own writing shape. The useful idea can keep working without starting from a blank box again.

That source-to-angle loop is the part I care about more than adding another calendar to the internet.
