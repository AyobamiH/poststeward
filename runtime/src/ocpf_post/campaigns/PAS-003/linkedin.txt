An AI handover should still make sense after the chat is closed.

What changed?
Which checks actually ran?
What still needs a decision?

Those are the questions I want a handover to answer before someone else has to reconstruct the work.

Proof & State separates preparing changes from checking the evidence. DoneState handles bounded repository work; OpsTruth inspects the evidence without repairing the system it checks.

That is the distinction I want teams to be able to use, not just another confident "done".
