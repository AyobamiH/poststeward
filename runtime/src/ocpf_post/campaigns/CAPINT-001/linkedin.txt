“Installed” is a weak answer when an agent needs to decide what it can actually use.

Capability Intelligence keeps capability lifecycle states separate:

discovered
→ present
→ installed
→ enabled
→ authenticated
→ runnable
→ verified

It also surfaces authority and evidence gaps instead of guessing readiness.

That makes the inventory useful for agent decisions rather than just a list of tools.
