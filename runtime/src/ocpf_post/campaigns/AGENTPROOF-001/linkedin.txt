When an agent changes a repository, “it succeeded” is not much of a handover.

The consequential action should leave evidence that can be checked later:

what was prepared
what authority approved it
what changed
whether retry was exactly-once
what signed receipt binds the result

That is the problem AgentProof is working on.

The current release-candidate implementation proves a bounded repository-patch flow and offline receipt verification, but I am not presenting it as a hosted production authority or generally available service yet.
