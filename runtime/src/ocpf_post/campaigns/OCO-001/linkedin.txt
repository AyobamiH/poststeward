OpenClaw can be the front door without every specialist workflow becoming a black box.

That is the direction behind OpenClaw Operator.

OpenClaw handles the generic shell and messaging experience. The Operator layer stays focused on bounded specialist work where approvals, run history, incidents and auditable actions matter.

The goal is not another generic dashboard.

It is making specialist operational work easier to delegate without losing the state you need to inspect later.
