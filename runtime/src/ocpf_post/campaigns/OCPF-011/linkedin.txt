Before asking AI for ten more posts, give it one thing worth saying.

The Lovable lesson I'm applying to content is simple: a vague starting point leaves the tool guessing what you meant.

"Write something about my business" is a blank brief.
A real customer question, a useful Reddit answer or a lesson from a build gives the draft a point.

Then decide:
Who is this for?
What should they understand?
What should they do next?

That is the direction I'm building OneClickPostFactory around: useful source material first, platform-native drafts second, review before distribution.

Not a promise that more AI output creates demand. A workflow worth testing with founders who already have something useful to say.
