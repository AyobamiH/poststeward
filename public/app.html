                      3. Prepare a campaign from a release
                    

                    

                      Turn release evidence into an audience-specific strategy
                      and original drafts. Review the sources, edit each variant
                      and approve the exact text. Preparation never publishes or
                      schedules it.
                    

                    

                      Bring your own OpenAI or Cloudflare AI account. The
                      selected owner account funds inference; a ChatGPT
                      subscription is separate. Keys stay encrypted in your
                      workspace and are never shared with agents or included in
                      drafts.
                    

                    
                    

                      No model call is made until you connect an account and
                      request preparation.
                    

                    

                      Connect or change your model account
                      

                        

                          Validation reads account/model/gateway metadata only.
                          It never runs a model, creates a gateway, changes
                          billing or tops up credits. Saving settings fences
                          queued work; regenerate it explicitly.
                        

                        
                        
                        

                          
                          
                          
                          

                            Use a dedicated key-free gateway with automatic
                            retries disabled for prepaid OpenAI. Gateway Run
                            tokens can reach every gateway in their account.
                            Shared balances are not exclusive workspace credits;
                            credit purchases carry Cloudflare's 5% fee. No
                            hidden service funding or automatic top-up.
                          

                          
                          
                          
                          
                          
                          
                          
                          
                          
                          

                            Fallback shares this Cloudflare account and the
                            chosen gateway for credit funding. Only the
                            explicitly selected route may follow a definite
                            model rejection, rate limit or invalid output.
                            Uncertain calls, credential rejection and policy
                            failures never trigger another paid attempt. Private
                            preparation is retained until reviewed
                            export/removal; immutable campaign and
                            operation/usage evidence remain.
                          

                        

                        
                        
                        
                        

                          Each full preparation reserves up to three calls. No
                          automatic retry follows an uncertain call. Limits
                          persist across reconnects. Add current provider rates
                          to obtain USD estimates and enforce an optional USD
                          budget.
                        

                        
                        
                        
                        
                      

                      
                    

                    

                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      
                      

                        Generation is a paid API operation against the selected
                        owner account, within saved limits. Connection
                        validation does not generate content.
                      

                      
                    

                    

                      Sources are bounded and pinned to a commit. Large or
                      incomplete releases show coverage gaps. Model checks are
                      assistance, not independent factual verification: inspect
                      source support before approval.
