OWNER-ONLY RECOVERY

Recover state without forgetting external effects.

Exact checkpoints restore PostSteward state. They do not erase what may have already happened at a social provider, so publication stays quarantined until restored authority is reconciled.

Open workspace recovery

EXACT CHECKPOINT CONTRACT

Exact recovery checkpoints

A checkpoint captures an exact Cloudflare Durable Object bookmark plus non-secret release and state-summary metadata. Raw bookmarks stay server-side and are never exposed in the browser.

Preparing a restore quarantines publication. It does not execute the restore. Execution still requires the exact typed workspace confirmation in the main workspace.
