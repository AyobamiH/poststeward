OWNER-ONLY RECOVERY

Recover state without forgetting external effects.

Exact checkpoints can restore PostSteward state. They cannot erase what may already have happened at a social provider, so publishing stays quarantined until authority and external evidence are reconciled.

EXACT CHECKPOINT CONTRACT

Exact recovery checkpoints

A checkpoint captures an exact Cloudflare Durable Object bookmark plus non-secret release and state-summary metadata. Raw bookmarks stay server-side.

Preparing a restore quarantines publication. It does not execute the restore; execution still requires exact typed workspace confirmation in the main workspace.
