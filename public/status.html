How to interpret this page

  • live_verified means reviewed external/runtime evidence exists for the stated scope.
  • external_setup_required means code alone is insufficient and a real provider/infrastructure step remains.
  • blocked_external means a dependency outside PostSteward is currently blocking the accepted path.
  • disabled_policy means the feature is deliberately off, not accidentally broken.
  • unavailable_capability means the runtime/client does not expose the required capability.
