IRREVERSIBLE OWNER ACTION

        
Delete this PostSteward workspace

        

          This is irreversible inside PostSteward. Deletion first
          installs a durable D1 tombstone and quarantines provider writes, then clears
          the workspace Durable Object and identity, grant, payment-mapping, recovery
          and external-effect records. The minimal workspace tombstone remains so stale
          restored state cannot be reactivated.
        

        

          Deletion expires open checkout sessions and cancels this workspace’s recurring
          subscriptions immediately, without a new invoice or automatic refund. Billing
          records remain until Stripe confirms cancellation. If verification fails,
          deletion stays pending and can be retried.
        

        

          Provider-side authorization grants may remain at the provider until revoked
          there, but PostSteward deletes its locally usable credential material and can
          no longer use this workspace. A later Google sign-in may create a new
          workspace; it does not reactivate this one.
