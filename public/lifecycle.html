Delete this PostSteward workspace

This is irreversible inside PostSteward. Deletion first installs a durable D1 tombstone and quarantines provider writes, then clears the workspace Durable Object and identity, grant, payment-mapping, recovery and external-effect records. The minimal workspace tombstone remains so stale restored state cannot be reactivated.

Provider-side authorization grants may remain at the provider until revoked there, but PostSteward deletes its locally usable credential material and can no longer use this workspace. A later Google sign-in may create a new workspace; it does not reactivate this one.
