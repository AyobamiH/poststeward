THE CONTROL PLANE

Automation with an explicit consequence model.

PostSteward is not a content generator. It sits between approved intent and social-provider effects, preserving identity, authority, timing and evidence around every consequential operation.

AUTHORITY

Give an agent only the operations it needs.

Owner-issued grants are scoped, expiring and revocable. Publishing, scheduling, automation and billing remain separate capabilities.

EFFECTS

Uncertainty does not become a duplicate post.

Idempotency, durable claims and ambiguous-effect preservation stop a transport failure from silently becoming a second provider write.

EVIDENCE

Inspect what the provider actually returned.

Receipt states keep reservations, creation IDs, exact readback, failures and drift separate so operators can reason from evidence.

FREE AND ADVANCED
